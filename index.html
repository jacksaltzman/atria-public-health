Who We Are

Our Mission

Atria Public Health is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit working to ensure that advances in preventive medicine reach everyone, not just the privileged few.

Through strategic partnerships with leading health organizations, universities, and community groups, we implement evidence-based programs that prevent chronic disease and strengthen health systems in underserved communities.

We believe that modern medicine—when combined with health education and community engagement—has the power to save and improve lives where it matters most.

Compassionate Care

Cardiovascular Health

RESTORE Health Equity Research Network

A groundbreaking collaboration between eight universities focused on reducing racial inequalities in cardiovascular disease outcomes. RESTORE translates evidence-based hypertension prevention into community settings, implementing findings directly in Black communities across the country.

  • 8 university research partners
  • Evidence-based hypertension prevention
  • Community-centered implementation
Community Outreach

CLIP: Community-to-Clinic Linkage Program

In partnership with the American Heart Association and NYU Grossman School of Medicine, CLIP brings blood pressure screenings to barbershops and community organizations on Staten Island. Beyond screening, we provide educational materials and connect residents with community health resources.

  • Barbershop-based health screenings
  • American Heart Association partnership
  • Direct clinic referral pathways
